OUR GREATEST ASSET: OUR PEOPLE

As RCD Group celebrated its 39th Anniversary, Boss Rolando C. Delantar, RCD Group Chairman and RCD Land President & CEO, placed the spotlight where he believes it belongs: on the people who continue to build the RCD Family.

Author: Fernyl Rebibis   Category: 39th Anniversary   Date: August 16, 2026

In his anniversary message, Boss RCD reminded the entire company that beyond properties, equipment, and accomplishments, its greatest asset has always been its people. “When we achieve success, let us not celebrate individually, let us celebrate as one family. Because I firmly believe that our greatest asset is not our properties, equipment; our greatest asset is our people.” His message was both a recognition of the people behind RCD's journey and a challenge for everyone to raise the bar—not only for the company, but for themselves.

DREAM BIGGER. WORK HARDER. TAKE OWNERSHIP.

Boss RCD called on every member of the RCD Family to aim higher, take greater responsibility, and believe in what can be accomplished through collective effort. “Dream bigger. Work Harder. Raise your standards, and take ownership. Never underestimate what we can accomplish when we move as one. Because RCD Family is not just a company, we are a team and a family; we are builders of dreams, we are builders of the future.” The message reflects the values that continue to shape the organization: loyalty, hard work, trust, ownership, and belief in one shared vision.

HONORING THE PEOPLE WHO BUILT WITH US

The anniversary celebration was also a tribute to the people whose dedication and commitment have helped shape RCD through the years. Boss RCD recognized the contributions of the managers, employees, and salespersons who continue to help build not only RCD Group, but also the future of the families and communities they serve. Among the night's meaningful moments was the recognition of 13 employees who have dedicated 5, 10, and 20 years of service to RCD Group. Each was honored with their respective Loyalty Ring, symbolizing years of commitment, trust, and shared growth. The company's top-performing salespersons were also recognized and awarded for their outstanding contributions to RCD Land's continued success.

IN PHOTO: Ar. Reynaldo De Leon receives his Loyalty Ring from Boss RCD, commemorating 20 years of dedication, hard work, and shared growth with the RCD Group.

39 YEARS IS ONLY THE FOUNDATION

While the anniversary honored the journey, Boss RCD reminded everyone that 39 years should not be a reason to become comfortable. For him, 39 years is not the destination—it is only the foundation. The next chapter, he emphasized, must be bigger. With the real estate industry, customers, technology, and competition constantly changing, RCD must also be ready to evolve.

“The next chapter of the RCD Family must be bigger than the rest. The real estate industry is changing, customers are changing, technology is changing, competition is becoming stronger; therefore, we must also change, we must innovate, we must move faster, we must become more professional, and most importantly we must work together.” With the right people, the right attitude, and one shared vision, Boss RCD believes RCD can continue to reach greater heights.

IN PHOTO: A moment of camaraderie among the RCD Land Inc. managers as they celebrate the company’s 39th Anniversary.

LOOKING FORWARD TO A BIGGER CHAPTER

As the company looks toward its 40th year, the challenge is clear: look forward, not backward. Be grateful for the people who built the foundation, but have the courage to build what comes next.

The 39th Anniversary was therefore more than a celebration of the past. It was a call for the entire RCD Family to take ownership of the future—to dream bigger, work harder, raise their standards, embrace change, and continue moving forward as one.

IN PHOTO: Salespersons from RCD Realty Marketing – Eagle Division capture a memorable moment with Boss RCD, celebrating 39 years of building, growing, and achieving success together as one RCD Family.

39 YEARS OF BUILDING DREAMS.

Established in 1987 by Boss Rolando C. Delantar, RCD Group was founded with a purpose: to help improve the lives of every Filipino through affordable and quality homes and communities across Batangas, Cavite, Bataan, and Bulacan.

Nearly four decades later, that vision continues to grow. 39 years of building dreams. 39 years of building communities. 39 years of improving lives.

And as one RCD Family, the best is still ahead.
